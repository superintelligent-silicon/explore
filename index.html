How it works The lab
01 — What SI means here

Not a bigger model. A cleaner signal.

Superintelligence, for us, is not a scoreboard. It is the practice of turning overwhelming input into small, durable outputs — open source when possible, modern best practices always, designed with the calm of something built to last.

The goal is practical: help humans improve their craft and their lives without wasting years on hype. Dense usefulness on a small footprint. Apple-level care. No fluff.

02 — The funnel

Information in. Product out.

  1. Intake News, tools, research, field notes.
  2. Triage Keep, test, or discard — with a reason.
  3. Refine Strip jargon. Rank. Note failure modes.
  4. Ship Guides, rankings, playbooks, signposts.

Default posture: reject most things. If we cannot name who it helps, it does not ship.
