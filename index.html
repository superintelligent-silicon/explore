How Explore works

Information in. Product out.

Our job is to condense useful knowledge for people, and to share it openly when we can.

  1. 01IntakeNews, tools, research, field notes.
  2. 02TriageKeep, test, or discard, with a reason.
  3. 03RefineStrip jargon. Rank. Note failure modes.
  4. 04ShipGuides, films, rankings, playbooks, open tools.

What SI means here

Not a bigger model. A cleaner signal.

Superintelligence, for us, is not a scoreboard. It is the practice of turning overwhelming input into small, durable outputs: open source when possible, modern best practices always, designed with the calm of something built to last.

The goal is practical: help people improve their craft and their lives without wasting years on hype. Dense usefulness on a small footprint.

Default posture: reject most things. If we can't name who it helps, it doesn't ship.
