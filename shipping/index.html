What’s shipping

Honest snapshot. No vapor. If it isn’t listed under Live, it isn’t live yet.

Live

  • Public home exploresuperintelligence.online — illustrated books, Threshold, research, SIOS lab, funnel, shipping.
  • Short alias exploresi.online redirects here.
  • Lab / org shell superintelligentsilicon.com — public brand; private ops stay private.
  • Design system v4 Restrained editorial system with light and dark themes, shared across the Explore Superintelligence home, Articles and About.
  • Ops model Intake → triage (keep / test / discard) → refine → ship. Default: reject most things.
  • Articles Articles — the interactive guide Defrost Mode and four illustrated, page-turning books: We’ll Be Right Back, Napkins Are the Backbone, The Final Merge and Immortal Tombstones, each with a TL;DR and read-aloud, and the prayer In Each Other.
  • Threshold Threshold: a field guide to superintelligence — interactive field guide in five chapters.
  • Research The Word Swap: from Artificial to Super Intelligence — interactive guide. All guides: /research/.
  • SIOS preview SIOS (Silicon OS / Superintelligent OS) — early, local-first in-browser desktop preview: open the live shell. Open source (MIT); Keys is not audited. Not a product download yet.
  • Contact Contact form on the org site, or exploresi@superintelligentsilicon.com.
  • About /about/ — how Explore Superintelligence relates to Grok Ai Apps (demo / entertainment support).
  • Source Public site: github.com/superintelligent-silicon/explore

Coming

  • Guides Short, durable how-tos after real triage — not blog noise.
  • Tool rankings Ordered shortlists with criteria you can argue with.
  • Signposts “Point here, not there” — skip hype, use what works.
  • Playbooks Repeatable sequences from input to outcome.
  • SIOS (lab) Next SIOS phases — module polish, Keys hardening on an audit path, and an optional small desktop wrapper. Roadmap: GitHub.

Not shipping

  • On purpose News firehose, affiliate mills, model-race scoreboards, public org charts, unfinished products dressed as launches.
