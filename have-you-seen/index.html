Everything here is code

Nothing on this page is a video. Every shape is a cloud of points generated from math, every transition is those points flying to their next shape under simple physics, and the music is composed and played live by your browser.

Drag to turn the model. Move your finger or mouse through it to push particles aside. Tap anywhere on it to send a wave of colour spreading through the cloud from that spot, with a note in the chapter's key.

Gold marks the human part of each story, cyan the model's. The science chapters cite their sources; the everyday chapters describe things anyone can try with an AI assistant today, and anything important is still worth checking with a person who knows.
