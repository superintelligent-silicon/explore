01 · What happened

A night stuck in defrost

Each store's fridges run on one small control computer that repair firms reach online. Attackers got in and set it to defrost all night.

−20°C
−4°F · freezer
−20°C0°20°50°C · ~6 h at Intermarché
  • Oct 11–12

    Overnight, fridges at Intermarché, Coopérative U, Carrefour, Monoprix, Leclerc and others are switched to defrost and left there.

  • Oct 12

    Paris prosecutors open an inquiry, citing spoiled food and fires. Danfoss, which makes the controls, says the hit units ran old or unsupported software.

  • Oct 13

    France's cyber-emergency team adds two more makers, Copeland and Dixell. About 700 of France's 10,000 supermarkets were hit, the finance minister says.

  • Oct 16

    Its updated advice: allow remote access only over a private, encrypted link, and check defrost schedules.

  • Oct 17

    One Normandy store's freezers reached 80°C, and nobody has claimed the attack, the Guardian reports.
