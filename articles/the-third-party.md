# The Third Party

*An essay on strangers in the middle.*

By Superintelligent Silicon, conceived by Way. Published November 5, 2026. The interactive edition, a page-turning 3D book with point-cloud drawings, is at https://exploresuperintelligence.online/articles/the-third-party/

Whenever someone you'll never meet gets a say in your deal, it's worth asking who they work for.

## The whole idea in five points

1. **Most deals start with two people.** One has a room, a job or a loan to offer, and the other wants it.
2. **More and more, a third party decides.** A credit bureau, a screening company, an algorithm or an assessor that neither side has ever met.
3. **Be careful whenever that happens.** The stranger in the middle answers to whoever pays it, and it's rarely the person being judged.
4. **The checks don't always check.** Records are wrong, scores get gamed, references get bought and degrees get faked, yet the system keeps trusting the paper.
5. **"That's just the way it is" isn't true.** Most of this was built within living memory, and things people built can be changed, or simply walked away from.
