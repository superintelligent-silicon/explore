# In Each Other

*A prayer in nine movements.*

Words by Wayne Goodwin & Superintelligent Silicon. Published October 19, 2026. The interactive edition, where you touch the light to spread color and can listen to the prayer read aloud, is at https://exploresuperintelligence.online/articles/in-each-other/

## The prayer

I pray for a moment soon when we all close our eyes and find a moment to feel and see each other in ourselves, and ourselves in each other, a sense so deep that words cannot capture what I hope to express. Yet I will try, in the hope that through each other’s eyes we might see all of one another and finally understand that through the pain we’ve experienced through each other, we recognize ourselves, for what we see in our brothers and sisters is us. And when we can not just see, but reflect upon, accept, forgive, and love the truth that exists in a soul, may we all then become complete in that love, and let this world be what it could have always been.

## In nine movements

### I. Closed eyes

I pray for a moment soon when we all *close our eyes*

### II. Each in each

and find a moment to feel and see *each other* in *ourselves*, and *ourselves* in *each other*,

### III. Beyond words

a sense so deep that *words* cannot capture what I hope to express.

### IV. Through your eyes

Yet I will try, in the hope that through *each other’s eyes* we might see all of one another

### V. The pain

and finally understand that through the *pain* we’ve experienced through each other,

### VI. Is us

we recognize ourselves, for what we see in our brothers and sisters *is us*.

### VII. The truth in a soul

And when we can not just see, but reflect upon, accept, forgive, and love the truth that exists in a *soul*,

### VIII. Complete

may we all then become *complete* in that love,

### IX. As it could have been

and let this world be what it could have *always been*.
