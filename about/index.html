About

Research here.
Play over there.

Explore Superintelligence is the public research face of Superintelligent Silicon — sourced, plain-language guides that compress useful knowledge about intelligence and the tools shaping it. Information in. Refined product out.

Two sides of the same orbit

  • Explore Research, explainers, and lab notes on this site — free to read, built to be cited. Start with Threshold, our interactive field guide to superintelligence, or browse Research, including The Word Swap.
  • Grok Ai Apps The demo and entertainment side — grokaiapps.com and Desk — lighter experiments and playable tools that help bring Explore to life.

How support works

Explore is brought to you with support through Grok Ai Apps. Optional tips run through that project’s Stripe so the books stay with the side that helps fund the research — not ads, not paywalls on the guides themselves.

  • Now Visit Grok Ai Apps or Desk to see the demo side.
  • Tip If Explore’s research has been useful, you can support it — $1 / $3 / $5. One-time tips, processed by Stripe through Grok Ai Apps.
  • Always Core research stays free and uncluttered. No popups, no sticky tip bars, no mid-article prompts.
